# How to install Smiteless

A simple guide. If you can double-click and copy-paste, you can do this. Takes about 2 minutes.

## What you need
- A Windows 10 or 11 computer.
- That's it. You do **not** need to install Python, AutoHotkey, or anything else — it's all inside the setup file.

---

## Step 1 — Download the setup file
1. Go to **https://github.com/bobbyroylee/smiteless/releases/latest**
2. Under **Assets**, click **`SmitelessSetup.exe`** to download it.
3. Wait for it to finish downloading (bottom of your browser, or your Downloads folder).

## Step 2 — Run it
1. Double-click **`SmitelessSetup.exe`**.
2. **Windows will probably show a blue "Windows protected your PC" box.** This is normal for new programs. Click **"More info"**, then click **"Run anyway"**.

   > *(It says that because the program is brand new and not from a big company — it's safe.)*
3. A small window opens. Click the **Install** button.
4. When it says **Done!**, click **Finish**.

That's it — Smiteless is installed and running. ✅

---

## Step 3 — Using it
- Look for a little **gold "S"** icon near the clock (bottom-right of your screen). If you don't see it, click the small **`^`** arrow next to the clock — it's hiding there. You can drag it out to keep it visible.
- **It opens by itself** when you're in champ select and in a game.
- To open it any time, press **`Ctrl` + `Alt` + `X`**.
- The **item helper** (a small window that tells you what to build) opens by itself in-game, or press **`Ctrl` + `Alt` + `B`**. You can drag it anywhere.
- **Important:** set League to **Borderless** mode (Settings → Graphics → Window Mode → Borderless), or the overlay won't show over the game.
- Smiteless **starts automatically** every time you turn on your computer.

---

## Step 4 (optional) — Unlock rank, win-streaks & duo detection
Out of the box you get champion builds, matchup tips, the item helper, and gank ratings.
To **also** see everyone's rank, recent win/loss streak, mastery, and who's duo'd, you need a
free Riot key (it's free and takes 2 minutes). Each person needs their own.

1. Open **https://developer.riotgames.com** in your browser.
2. Click **Login** (top-right) and sign in with your normal League/Riot account.
3. After logging in you'll see a box called **"Development API Key"**. Click **Regenerate API Key** if it's empty.
4. Click **Copy** next to the key (it looks like `RGAPI-xxxxxxxx-xxxx-...`).
5. Open Smiteless (press **`Ctrl` + `Alt` + `X`**). At the **bottom** of the window there's a key bar — click **Paste**, then **Save**.
6. Done. It works in your next game.

> ⚠️ This free key **stops working after 24 hours** and you'll need to repeat steps 1–5. If
> that's annoying, you can apply for a free **"Personal" key** on the same site that never
> expires — ask whoever sent you this for help, or just re-paste a new key each day.

---

## When there's an update
When a new version comes out, a little **notification pops up from your system tray** (near the
clock) that says **"Smiteless update available"**. It checks on its own — when you're playing,
and every few hours. To install it, **right-click the gold S icon** and click **"Update to v…"**.
It downloads and installs by itself, then restarts. (You can also check any time: right-click the
**S** → **Check for updates**.)

## To uninstall
Open **Start menu → Smiteless → Uninstall Smiteless** (or Windows **Settings → Apps → Smiteless → Uninstall**).

---

*Trouble? The gold S icon → right-click → it has Open overlay, Item widget, Settings, and Reload.
Make sure League is in **Borderless** mode if the overlay isn't showing over your game.*
